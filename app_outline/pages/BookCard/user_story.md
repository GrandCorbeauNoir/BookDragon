# User book card: 



## On this page

- **BookDragon** logo with the book dragon picture to the left of **BookDragon** should appear in scarlet red at the top, left side of page.
- **Books** The results of a book search appear with 20 modal images per page, in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
**Navigation Bar** At the top of the page and centered, from left to right appear the clickable words Home, Genres with a drop-down list of the genres: Adventure, Technology, Horror, Sci-Fi, Fiction, Non-Fiction and finally a search bar. Clicking on any of the words or the search bar icon performs a search and the results of the search will appear with 20 modal images per page appear in rows of 5 by columns of 4 with the number of pages of results listed at the center of the bottom of the page.
**Load More** button at the bottom of page that when clicked, will load additional book results.
**Background** a page background picture will be provided.
